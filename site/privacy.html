2. In brief

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and embeds no third-party content, except for the map tiles from OpenStreetMap (section 4). Fonts, scripts and icons are served from our own server.

3. Server log files

When you visit the website, our web server processes technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, requested file, HTTP status code, amount of data transferred, referrer and browser identification (user agent). The purpose is to deliver the website and to keep it secure and stable. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure operation).

Retention: log files are rotated daily and deleted automatically after no more than 15 days, unless a specific security incident requires longer retention for its investigation.

Hosting: the website runs on a server operated by Nessus GmbH in Austria. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with the provider.

4. Map display (OpenStreetMap)

To display the map, your browser loads map tiles directly from the servers of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (St John's Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom). In doing so, your IP address, your browser identification and the address of this page (referrer) are transmitted to the OpenStreetMap Foundation. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a working map display). The transfer to the United Kingdom is based on the adequacy decision of the European Commission. Further information: privacy policy of the OpenStreetMap Foundation.

5. Contact by email

If you email us, we process your details to handle your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed for that purpose, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

6. External links

The source links and map links in the popups lead to third-party websites. Data is transmitted to the respective provider only when you click such a link.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). To exercise these rights, please contact the email address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Austria this is the Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40-42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at.
